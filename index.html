What you're seeing

The flat map colors each county by who won it, no matter how many people voted there. A county where 67 votes were cast is painted just as boldly as one where 1.38 million were cast.

Press Raise the counties and each county becomes a column whose height is the number of votes cast there in the Senate race. Most of the red area is land where few people vote, and the tallest columns are in the cities.
